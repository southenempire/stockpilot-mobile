## 🎯 Executive Summary
**StockPilot Mobile** is an autonomous 24/7 AI stock robo-advisor designed natively for the **Solana Seeker** phone and the **Solana dApp Store**. It eliminates the friction of traditional brokerage market hours and manual portfolio rebalancing by letting users invest in non-custodial, thematic stock baskets on Solana with hardware-grade security powered by the **Solana Mobile Stack (SMS)** and **Seed Vault**.
